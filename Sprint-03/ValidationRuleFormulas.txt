=========================================
Validation Rule 1
=========================================

Rule:
Student CGPA must be greater than or equal to the Job's minimum CGPA.

Formula:
CGPA__c < Minimum_CGPA__c

=========================================
Validation Rule 2
=========================================

Rule:
Application Date cannot be after the Job Closing Date.

Formula:
Application_Date__c > Job_Closing_Date__c

=========================================
Validation Rule 3
=========================================

Rule:
Mandatory fields cannot be left blank.

Formula:
OR(
ISBLANK(Student__c),
ISBLANK(Job__c)
)
